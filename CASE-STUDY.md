# CASE STUDY — SOLUSI KEMENAG

## CARD DESCRIPTION

SOLUSI KEMENAG — OFFICIAL PUBLIC-SERVICE PORTAL OF KEMENTERIAN AGAMA KOTA PROBOLINGGO. ONE PLATFORM FOR ONLINE SERVICE APPLICATIONS, LIVE PROGRESS TRACKING, COMPLAINTS, AND AN AI-POWERED HELP DESK, BUILT FOR WBK/WBBM BUREAUCRACY REFORM.

## CASE-STUDY BRIEF INTRO

KEMENAG KOTA PROBOLINGGO NEEDED TO MOVE FACE-TO-FACE, PAPER-BASED SERVICES ONLINE WITHOUT LEAVING ANYONE BEHIND. THE RESULT IS A SINGLE ACCESSIBLE PORTAL WHERE CITIZENS SUBMIT APPLICATIONS, TRACK THEM IN REAL TIME, AND GET INSTANT ANSWERS — WHILE EVERY STEP IS TIMESTAMPPED FOR CLEAN SLA AUDITS.

## THE BRIEF

- DIGITIZE PUBLIC SERVICES INTO ONE OFFICIAL, TRUSTWORTHY PORTAL
- REPLACE WALK-IN PAPERWORK WITH ONLINE SUBMISSION + REAL-TIME STATUS TRACKING
- GIVE STAFF AN ADMIN DASHBOARD WITH A PER-STEP SLA AUDIT TRAIL
- PROVIDE 24/7 SELF-SERVICE THROUGH AN AI ASSISTANT AND WHATSAPP CENTER
- MEET WBK/WBBM REFORM STANDARDS WITH INCLUSIVE, WCAG-ALIGNED ACCESSIBILITY

## DESIGN

- EDITORIAL CREAM CANVAS WITH A DRIFTING GREEN GRID — OFFICIAL YET APPROACHABLE
- GREEN AS THE PRIMARY ACTION COLOR, YELLOW AS HIGH-ENERGY ACCENT, INK FOR TYPE
- CARD-BASED MODULES: SERVICE CATALOG WITH INSTANT SEARCH, NEWS, REGULATIONS, PPID
- FLOATING ACTION HUB: ACCESSIBILITY PANEL, WHATSAPP CENTER, AI ASSISTANT — ONE TAP AWAY
- ACCESSIBILITY-FIRST: FONT SCALING, DARK MODE, READABLE MODE, NO-MOTION, TEXT-TO-SPEECH, COLORBLIND FILTERS
- ADMIN SIDE: CHART.JS VISUALS, TANSTACK TABLES, XLSX EXPORT, AND A NOTION-STYLE EDITORJS CMS
